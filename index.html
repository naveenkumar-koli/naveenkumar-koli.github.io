About Me

Who I Am

I'm an AI/ML Engineer based in Bangalore with 1.5+ years of experience building Python-based machine learning, NLP, and GenAI applications. I work across the full stack — OCR and document parsing, local and API-based LLMs, classic ML, and fine-tuned transformers.

I hold a Master's in Computer Science from Karnataka University, and I'm certified in Data Science (ExcelR) and Machine Learning with Python (IBM).
